# Bootcademy search ads and landing-page map

Updated: 2026-11-16

This is a launch structure, not a forecast. Validate Nigerian search volume, competition and bids in Google Keyword Planner before assigning budget. Google recommends tightly themed ad groups and landing pages that closely match each ad and keyword.

## Recommended launch campaigns

| Ad group         | Start with phrase/exact variants                                                              | Landing page                            | Core promise                                                              |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Academy software | academy management software; academy management system; training academy software             | `/academy-management-software`          | Run admissions, people, programmes, branches and finance in one workspace |
| Sports academy   | sports academy management software; sports academy software; player management system         | `/sports-academy-management-software`   | Manage players, coaches, sessions, attendance and fees                    |

Start with exact and phrase match around the high-intent commercial terms. Use the search-terms report to discover proven variants before expanding broad match.

## Negative-keyword starting list

Review before applying; do not blindly block a term that matches a real Bootcademy segment.

- jobs, salary, vacancy, career
- course, tutorial, certification, degree
- crack, nulled, pirated, download apk
- academy near me, football trials, player scouting
- school portal login, result checker
- free template, spreadsheet template, source code
- fixed asset depreciation, accounting standard, ERP

“Free” is not a universal negative because the academy website is an explicit offer. Keep it only in the website ad group and monitor whether those searches produce qualified academy owners.

## Ad-message guardrails

- Mirror the landing-page promise in the headline. Do not send a “football academy software” ad to a generic homepage when the dedicated football page is available.
- Use one primary conversion: account registration. Configure analytics to distinguish ad-group landing pages and completed registration.
- Do not advertise fixed-asset management, native mobile apps, full accounting, automated scouting, unlimited branches or unapproved discounts.
- “Paystack payments” means payments are verified through the implemented provider workflow. External processing fees and academy settlement arrangements remain separate.
- “Free academy website” means the connected academy website is included; custom implementation, domain work and external services are not silently promised.

## Measurement before scaling

1. Add Google Ads and analytics conversion events for registration start, registration completion and academy onboarding completion.
2. Preserve UTM parameters through registration and store only appropriate attribution data with a retention rule.
3. Run one tightly themed campaign at a time with Nigeria location targeting and the language settings that match the ad copy.
4. Review search terms, conversion rate, cost per completed onboarding, ad relevance and landing-page experience every week.
5. Pause terms that attract learners looking for courses, players looking for trials, or developers looking for source code.
